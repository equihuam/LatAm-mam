Direct

Temperature & heat stress

Heatwave mortality (bats, small mammals); reduced fecundity in carnivores/ungulates; den/roost overheating.

Cetacean thermoregulation stress; pup mortality in pinnipeds; range shifts to cooler waters.

Direct

Hydroclimatic extremes (drought/flood)

Vegetation loss → prey decline; collapse of water refuges; increased wildfire exposure; altered migration.

Reduced river discharge (river dolphins/manatees); inundation of haul-outs; flash-flood stranding.

Direct

Sea-level rise & cyclonic storms

Coastal refuge loss; saline intrusion into lowland forests; displacement from deltas and estuaries.

Rookery/beach loss; storm-driven pup mortality; haul-out erosion.

Ocean warming & acidification

Prey base decline & trophic mismatch; distribution shifts; altered foraging success.

Direct

Disease dynamics

Distemper (canids/mustelids), hantavirus in rodents; heat-facilitated parasite burdens.

Morbillivirus & toxoplasmosis in dolphins; harmful algal blooms.

●●●○○

Indirect

Habitat loss & fragmentation

Drought/fire → forest conversion; corridor loss for jaguars/tapirs; urban sprawl intensifies heat & barriers.

Mangrove/wetland loss reduces manatee shelter; coastal development blocks haul-outs.

●●●●●

Indirect

Prey-web shifts & trophic cascades

Vegetation/productivity changes reduce herbivore prey; cascading effects on carnivores; mesopredator release.

Fish stock collapse → dolphin/seal food scarcity; shifts concentrate whales near shipping lanes.

●●●●○

Indirect

Conflict & infrastructure

Drought → new dams/pipelines; roads expand with land-use change; retaliatory killing near farms.

Ports, seawalls, traffic disrupt movement; higher ship-strike & noise exposure as routes intensify.
